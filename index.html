01 / AN INTRODUCTION

A home for things
that took shape.

Most things here start with an ordinary problem, a stray thought or a joke that somehow becomes a project.

I make decorative objects, useful bits for the house, software tools and sometimes repurpose old hardware or try to make things glow. Some work out. Others are still being figured out.

This is a selection from the workshop, not a catalogue of finished products.

OBJECT / 001FORMING

Arcane Hub Reliquary

I wanted to hide a rather plain bit of computer equipment. The cover now involves a hinged door, a lot of printed tiles and a watchful eye.

Open the record ↗

The idea. Hide the equipment without making the space difficult to get into.

Where it is now. A planned cabinet screen with modular decorative panels and translucent sections; the door layout has to work around the hinge.

Still working on. Print quality, individual tile sizes and keeping the overall design lined up across the door.

VIEW FULL IMAGE ↗SLICER VIEW / CORNER SECTIONS
OBJECT / 002ADOPTED CORE

The Celtic Rail

A printed Celtic braid for the living room. Getting the repeat and the hidden joins right took most of the early work.

Open the record ↗

The idea. Make a matching decorative rail to run around the living room.

What worked. A standard section with a 220 mm installed pitch, hidden joins and repeatable braid detail.

What came next. Corners and feature panels. The card image is a slicer preview of the rail components; the Tree-of-Life panel has separate production renders.

OBJECT / 003PROOF

Unnecessary Triumphs

Fishing awards for achievements that probably don’t merit civic monuments. So I designed some anyway.

Open the record ↗

The idea. Three deliberately over-serious awards for a fishing trip, plus a commemorative token.

Where it is now. The Surmullet and Present at Events have engineered print packages and previews.

Still working on. Print presentation and checking which photographs and likenesses can be published. The selected picture is design artwork rather than evidence of a finished print.

WORLD / 001FORMING

Little Hub Person

A small resident for a household console, with a room to live in, jobs to do and a book to read.

Open the record ↗

The idea. Make the quiet moments on a home console feel inhabited rather than empty.

Where it is now. The room and character designs are established. The first walking, sitting and reading activities are being developed.

Still working on. Turning the room and character artwork into believable small movements and everyday routines. This is scene artwork, not a runtime screenshot.

04 / FIELD NOTE 001

NOTES FROM THE LIVING ROOM

Making a Celtic rail
for the living room.

I wanted a black-and-copper Celtic braid to run along the walls. Printing it in pieces seemed the sensible way to do it.

The tricky part was making the pattern join cleanly, including at corners and around shorter gaps. Once the basic section worked, I started making a few different feature panels to fit the same system.

The straight rail is adopted. The Tree-of-Life panel in the picture is a newer production design.

05 / WHO'S MAKING ALL THIS?

A workshop for
curious detours.

Made From Fog is my place for making things. Some are 3D-printed objects, some are software, and some end up as a mixture of both.

I like trying an idea out, seeing where it falls short and making another version. This site collects the more interesting results, along with a few things still in progress.

It isn't a shop or a formal portfolio. At least, not yet.
