THE VISITORS' NOTEBOOK / PRIVACY

What happens to your note?

A small notebook, not a public comment feed.

Private by default

While the notebook's postbox is closed, this website does not accept notes. Once enabled, your note will go privately to the person behind Made From Fog, rather than appearing on the site. Name and reply email are optional. Please don't include sensitive personal information.

Why the information is collected

To read your message and respond if you request a reply. We collect the words you choose to send, any alias you supply, and your email only if you give one. Basic technical anti-spam information may also be processed by the delivery service.

Who handles delivery

When note delivery is activated, the visitors' notebook will use FormSubmit, a third-party form service that forwards submissions privately by email. See their privacy notice for information about their handling of submission data.

Could it be made public?

Only if you explicitly tick the optional publication permission. Even then, the site owner reviews it first and may decide not to publish it. If a note is selected, only the note and your chosen name or alias may be displayed. Your email address is never displayed. Nothing is published automatically.

Keeping or removing notes

Notes are kept only as long as reasonably needed to read, reply and decide whether to retain or publish them. You can request that a note be removed by replying to any email exchange with us or sending another note that identifies it. A public note may be removed on request.

No accounts or visitor tracking

There are no visitor accounts, comments database, analytics scripts or advertising trackers in the Made From Fog site itself. GitHub Pages and the form provider may process technical connection data to operate their services.
