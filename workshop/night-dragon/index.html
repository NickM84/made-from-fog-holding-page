INTERACTIVE ADVENTURE / PRIVATE REVIEW R4

Night Dragon.

An old-fashioned fantasy adventure in an unusually theatrical setting: the book is open, the dice are ready, and the next decision is yours.

A gamebook you can step into

The experience is presented as a great illustrated tome in a candlelit cathedral. Instead of a conventional game menu, the story lives on the pages. Dice, choices, equipment and campaign controls sit around the book.

Part of the appeal is the atmosphere: something closer to sitting down with a dramatic fantasy adventure book than clicking through a standard browser game.

What's running

The Night Dragon R4 Linux review build is running on an isolated local test installation. Its opening book, original Background passage, illustration, dice tray and campaign controls have been observed in a desktop browser.

That is a first visual launch, not a claim that the whole adventure has passed review. Gameplay progression, returning to saved campaigns, sounds and optional local narration still need their own hands-on checks.

Can I play it?

Not publicly yet. This is a private playtest, not a public download or an open server. When the experience is ready for visitors, it will use separate, appropriately secured game hosting rather than exposing the household workshop machine.

This record describes the review state on 23 October 2026. The decorative title study above is not the game's cover or a screenshot. A verified image and a public play link can be added separately when ready.
