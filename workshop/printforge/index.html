WORKSHOP TOOL / PRINTFORGE

PrintForge

A little software for keeping track of what was designed, what was printed, and whether the physical result was actually any good.

Why make another piece of software?

Because the printing part of a project isn't quite the same as the design part. A model might have several revisions, each with its own attempts. A successful print might still have a bad fit or a finish I don't want to keep.

PrintForge is a local-first application I'm making to keep those things separate, with the aim of doing less remembering and less copying between files and notes.

What's different about it?

A design revision is not a print attempt. A failed attempt doesn't automatically mean the design changed. And a successful attempt doesn't mean the finished object has been adopted. That sounds obvious until you're trying to piece the whole history together afterwards.

The application has Projects and standalone Items, revision history, print attempts and evidence records, with a review step after physical inspection. The initial real proof is built around the Fourfold Ward.

Where it stands

The V1 workflow foundation is under development. It has a React frontend, an Express backend and a local SQLite database. The project repository is private, and I'm not presenting this as a download or a finished public product.

It fits Made From Fog because making the tools for the workshop is part of the work, just like making the objects themselves.
